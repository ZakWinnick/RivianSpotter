## Description

A field guide to every Rivian place, and a private record of the ones you’ve been to.

RivSpotter puts the whole Rivian map in your pocket: Rivian Adventure Network chargers, Waypoints, Spaces, Service Centers, Demo Centers, and Outposts across the US and Canada. Browse them as a photo gallery, by state, by type, or by what’s there, like Plug & Charge, a trailer-friendly stall, or coffee while you wait. Every place has its address, directions, hours, charging details, and prices.

Check in when you visit and build your collection, with stats and a receipt card you can save or share. Add the widget to your Home Screen to check in with one tap, or to your Lock Screen to see the closest place. Pick a Base Camp, the place you think of as home. Your visits, notes, saved places, and Base Camp are private and sync through your own iCloud account.

New and coming-soon locations arrive automatically from RivSpotter.com. Works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, in Light or Dark.

RivSpotter is independent and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Rivian.

## What to Test (TestFlight, version 1.0)

Build 25 introduces RivSpotter and rivspotter.com. Verify that existing visits, notes, saved places, Base Camp, and iCloud sync survive the update, and check app and widget links. Build 24 also added distances to nearby places. Please try:
- Open a place and scroll to More Places. Check the distance labels and tap a suggestion, then go back.
- Try an upcoming location with an approximate pin. Distances should be marked “Approx.”; missing pins should show “Distance unavailable.”
- Distances are measured from the place you’re viewing, not your current location. No location permission is needed.

Also keep testing:
- Browsing All Locations in grid, thumbnails, and list, and sorting by Nearest.
- Opening a place, getting directions, and checking in. Then look for it in Lists and Visit Receipts.
- Choosing a Base Camp, and checking that it and your visits appear on your other devices.
- Settings: Light, Dark, and System appearance.
- Adding the Home Screen widget at a Rivian place and checking in, then confirming the visit appears in the app.

Tell us about anything that looks wrong or confusing through TestFlight feedback.
