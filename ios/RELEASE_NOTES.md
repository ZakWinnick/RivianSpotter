# What’s New in RivianSpotter

## Version 1.0

RivianSpotter 1.0 is here: a field guide to every Rivian place, and a private record of the ones you’ve been to.

**The whole map, in your pocket.** Rivian Adventure Network chargers, Waypoints, Spaces, Service Centers, Demo Centers, and Outposts across the US and Canada, each with its address, hours, charging details, prices, and directions.

**Built to be browsed.** Photo cards in a grid, thumbnails, or a list. Sort by name, state, or what’s nearest, filter down to exactly the stop you want, or explore by state, location type, charts, and the glossary.

**Your collection.** Check in when you visit, keep private notes, and watch your Visited count grow. Every visit becomes a receipt you can save or share, and Base Camp marks the place you call home.

**Check in from your Home Screen.** Small, medium, and Lock Screen widgets let you check in with one tap when you’re at a place, and show the closest place when you’re not. Optional arrival updates, off by default, get the widget ready as you arrive, and your location stays on your device.

**Private by design.** No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your visits sync only through your own iCloud. Read the details at rivianspotter.com/privacy.html.

**Everywhere you are.** iPhone, iPad, and Mac, in Light, Dark, or System appearance, with new and coming-soon locations arriving on their own.

## Version 0.2

A field guide to Rivian, built to be browsed.

**Browse like a field guide.** Every location is a photo card. Flip between a grid, big thumbnails, or a compact list, then sort by name, state, or what’s closest to you. Filter by location type, site feature, or state to find exactly the stop you’re after.

**Place pages, redone.** The name, type, and your visit status sit right under the photo. Site features like Plug & Charge, Trailer OK, and coffee nearby appear as chips; tap one to see what it means. Directions are right below the address, followed by hours, charging details, and prices.

**Lists and visit receipts.** See how many places you’ve been to and what share of the map that covers, broken down by type. Every check-in becomes a receipt card you can save or share. Your notes never leave the app.

**Base Camp.** Pick the place you think of as home, whether it’s your usual charger or your Service Center. It syncs privately across your devices with iCloud.

**More ways to explore.** Browse by state or province, see the whole directory in charts, and look up any location type or site feature in the glossary. On iPad, a sidebar keeps it all one tap away.

**Always current.** New and coming-soon locations arrive from RivianSpotter.com on their own, with richer charging details: prices, time-of-use schedules, individual chargers, and reliability grades.

**Your look, your call.** Choose Light, Dark, or System in Settings, at the bottom of Browse.

**Fixes.** Settings opens reliably when you run RivianSpotter on a Mac, and your Base Camp now sits neatly on its own line in the sidebar.

## Version 0.1
