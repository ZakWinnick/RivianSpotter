# What’s New in RivianSpotter

## Version 0.2

A field guide to Rivian, built to be browsed.

**Browse like a field guide.** Every location is a photo card. Flip between a grid, big thumbnails, or a compact list, then sort by name, state, or what’s closest to you. Filter by location type, site feature, or state to find exactly the stop you’re after.

**Place pages, redone.** The name, type, and your visit status sit right under the photo. Site features like Plug & Charge, Trailer OK, and coffee nearby appear as chips; tap one to see what it means. Directions are right below the address, followed by hours, charging details, and prices.

**Lists and visit receipts.** See how many places you’ve been to and what share of the map that covers, broken down by type. Every check-in becomes a receipt card you can save or share. Your notes never leave the app.

**Base Camp.** Pick the place you think of as home, whether it’s your usual charger or your Service Center. It syncs privately across your devices with iCloud.

**More ways to explore.** Browse by state or province, see the whole directory in charts, and look up any location type or site feature in the glossary. On iPad, a sidebar keeps it all one tap away.

**Always current.** New and coming-soon locations arrive from RivianSpotter.com on their own, with richer charging details: prices, time-of-use schedules, individual chargers, and reliability grades.

**Your look, your call.** Choose Light, Dark, or System in Settings, at the bottom of Browse.

## Version 0.1

Hello, world. RivianSpotter arrives on iPhone and iPad.

- Explore every Rivian Adventure Network site, Waypoint, Space, Service Center, Demo Center, and Outpost.
- Check in when you visit, add a private note, and look back on everywhere you’ve been.
- Save places for later, see what’s nearby, and get directions in Apple Maps.
- Your journal stays private and syncs with iCloud.
