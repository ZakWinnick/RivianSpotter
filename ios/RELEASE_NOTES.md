# RivianSpotter release notes

Newest first. Add an entry for every build that ships to TestFlight or the App Store, written for the people using the app. Build-by-build test evidence lives in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

## 0.2.0 (9): Lights, please

*Not yet uploaded.*

- **Choose your appearance.** Settings has a clear Light / Dark / System switch. Pick one and the whole app follows right away.
- **Settings is easier to find.** On iPhone, it’s one tap away from the gear in the corner of Locations, Lists, and Browse.

## 0.2.0 (8): The field guide update

*October 21, 2026*

RivianSpotter now feels like a proper field guide, with a layout inspired by Facades, Michael Steeber’s guide to Apple Stores.

- **Every location is a photo card.** Browse as a grid, big thumbnails, or a compact list. Sort by name, state, or what’s nearest, and filter by type, site feature, status, or state.
- **Place pages got a makeover.** Type and visit pills sit under the name, and site features appear as chips you can tap to learn what they mean. Directions are right under the address.
- **Lists.** See how many places you’ve visited and what share of the map that is, with your visits broken down by type.
- **Visit receipts.** Every check-in becomes a receipt card you can save or share. Your notes stay out of it.
- **Base Camp.** Choose the place you think of as home. It syncs across your devices with iCloud.
- **More ways to browse:** States & Provinces, Location Data charts, and a glossary of location types and site features.
- **On iPad,** a full sidebar puts every collection one tap away.

## 0.2.0 (5–7): Fresh directions

*October 20, 2026*

- **The map keeps itself current.** New and coming-soon locations arrive from rivianspotter.com while you use the app, and keep working offline once downloaded.
- **Richer place details.** Charging prices, time-of-use schedules, individual chargers, reliability grades, and site features, presented the same way as on the website.
- **Map filters** for type, status, state, and saved places.
- Plenty of polish from testing, including clearer buttons and smoother iPad browsing.

## 0.1.0 (1–4): Hello, world

*October 20, 2026*

The first beta. RivianSpotter arrives on iPhone and iPad.

- Browse every Rivian Adventure Network site, Waypoint, Space, Service Center, Demo Center, and Outpost, with photos where we have them.
- Check in to record a visit, add a private note, and look back at everywhere you’ve been.
- Save places for later, find what’s near you, and get directions in Apple Maps.
- Your journal syncs privately through iCloud.
- Follow-up builds tidied the bookmark icon, made the journal more resilient, kept map clusters from piling up, and let text scale with your Dynamic Type size.
