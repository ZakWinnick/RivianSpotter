Follow changes across the map

Oct 16, 20262026.42.26

Charging locations and map improvements

  • Added Rivian’s announced future charging locations, including King City and Salinas, with Coming Soon labels and distinct map markers.
  • Added daily updates for coming-soon locations. City-level pins show approximate locations until station addresses are published.
  • Coming-soon locations now carry their saved places and page links forward when a matching active station is identified, adding published station and charging details automatically.
  • Light and dark appearance now follows your device automatically, with no separate theme switch.
  • Closing location details now zooms out to the surrounding region while keeping that location centered.
  • Combined Rivian Adventure Network and Waypoint stations at matching addresses into shared listings, with separate charger counts, speeds, and published rates for each network.
  • Made charging prices easier to read, with compact price ranges and expandable time-of-use schedules on location pop-ups and detail pages.
