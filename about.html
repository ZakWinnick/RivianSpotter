Information from Rivian.

Location information comes from Rivian’s official location directory, its location pages, its service-center map, and its charging map. Location pages include official links and the latest source refresh date.

Opening hours use the destination’s local time zone. Visiting, service, and charging hours are listed separately where published. Service visits and demo drives can be arranged directly with Rivian.

This directory includes Rivian Adventure Network (RAN) and Waypoint charging locations from Rivian’s public charging map. Charger counts, published maximum power and access are shown on station pages. Map pins come from the official map; directions use the published station address when available. These are station details, not live charger availability. Charging details include pricing, connectors, location grades, and reported issues. Each snapshot has its own capture date. Charging information is refreshed daily. Nearby charging and Rivian places link to each other without merging their services or hours.

Privacy, in plain language.

Saved places and theme preferences stay in your browser. “Near me” requests your location only when you tap it; coordinates are used locally to sort places and are not saved by Rivian Spotter. The map provider receives normal map requests for the area you view.

Maps are provided by Mapbox and its data partners. Their privacy policy applies to map use. We use Tinylytics for site-usage analytics. We do not add advertising trackers. A service worker stores public pages and location data on your device for faster repeat visits and limited offline access.

Correction reports open an email draft in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it.
